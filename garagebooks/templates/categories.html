Tick GST only partly applies for a category where only part of an invoice carries GST, such as registration (the CTP part has GST, the registration fee doesn't). The GST isn't assumed to be 1/11 of the invoice: the amount read from the invoice, or entered on the expense, is used, and with none the expense is treated as having no GST. It only changes what an expense paid from a lease package is shown to cost after tax.

Tick Has a period for a category whose expenses cover a stretch of time, such as insurance and registration: the Add and Edit form and the import page then ask for the period's start and end dates, and read them from the document when they can.
