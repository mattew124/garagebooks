Tick GST-free for a category whose invoices carry no GST, such as registration and CTP. It only changes what an expense paid from a lease package is shown to cost after tax: the GST on an invoice that isn't GST-free is taken to be a flat 1/11 of it.

Tick Has a period for a category whose expenses cover a stretch of time, such as insurance and registration: the Add and Edit form and the import page then ask for the period's start and end dates, and read them from the document when they can.
