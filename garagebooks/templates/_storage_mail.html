{{ brand("outlook") }} Invoices by email

Invoices and refunds that arrive by email can be imported from an Outlook.com mailbox: choose the mail folder each car's emails are filed in, and its emails are offered on the same import page as a folder of PDFs. Each attached PDF is one expense, saved in the car's invoice storage; an email with no PDF, such as a refund notice, is read from its text.
