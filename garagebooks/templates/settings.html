Cost after tax

For each income year (1 July to 30 June), the tax bracket your taxable income falls in and your Medicare levy status. An expense's date decides its year, and its marginal rate is the bracket's rate plus the levy (2%, 1% when half exempt, none when exempt). It shows what an expense paid from a lease package costs after tax. A year left blank uses the latest earlier year's choice (for the income its bracket stands for). Only the bracket is kept, never a salary. With no year chosen, no after-tax figure is shown. This is arithmetic on what you choose, not tax advice.
